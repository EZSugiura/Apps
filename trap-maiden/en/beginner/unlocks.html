    Features by Floor

    "Clear B-number" means after clearing that floor for the first time. The contents of the gacha depend on your "reached stage" (the deepest floor you can currently enter: the deepest cleared floor + 1).

    Features opened by story progress

    FloorWhat opensNotes
    StartPractice floors B1 to B4Only the glowing button can be pressed. The first floor teaches moving, enemy movement and placing traps; the next teaches holding enemies; the next, luring; the last, COMBO and CHAIN.
    Clear B1Fortune Teller (Martha), Gordon's Workshop (Trap Gacha)You receive traps: Bear Trap ×3.
    Clear B2A gift of trapsYou receive Clapper ×2.
    Clear B3A gift of trapsYou receive Oil Pot ×3, Flint Trap ×3.
    Clear B4You can move around freely. Gear & Upgrades is explained (stock-up, level-up)See the table below for loadout slots.
    Clear B5Armor Shop (the shopkeeper), Armor Gacha introducedYou receive Hard Hat ×1.
    Clear B7A gift of trapsYou receive Vibration Stake ×2.
    Clear B8The Training Cave opensWatch Gordon's scene, then go to the Cave on the village map (Daily Routine).
    Clear B10Stacked placingPlace several of the same trap on one tile (see below).
    Clear B10Hard ModeSee Hard Mode.
    Clear B30Lily's House (living room)See Lily's House. A reward for rescuing Mia.
    Clear B30Skipping in the CaveSee Daily Routine.
    Clear B50The kitchen of Lily's House
    Clear B90The workshop of Lily's HouseMother and Father appear

    When loadout slots increase

    The number of different traps you can bring on a run (loadout slots) increases when you clear the floors below. Hard Mode set rewards and Cave milestone rewards raise it too. The maximum is 12.

    FloorLoadout slots
    Clear B4Up to 4
    Clear B40Up to 9
    Clear B60Up to 10
    Clear B80Up to 11
    Clear B100Up to 12

    Stacked placing (after clearing B10)

    • You can place several of the same trap on one tile. Choose the number with "- count +" in the trap menu.
    • One is used up each time the trap triggers.
    • Only traps you own two or more of can be stacked. A few traps (such as the wooden spike board) can only be placed one at a time.
    • Once B10 is cleared, it works in the story, Hard Mode and the Cave.

    Kinds of gacha

    There are four gachas: two for traps and two for armor.

    GachaCostWhere
    Trap Gacha (normal)Gems (100 for one, 450 for 5)Gordon's Workshop
    Trap Gacha (paid)Store purchase (check the price in the game)Gordon's Workshop
    Armor Gacha (normal)Gems (100 for one, 450 for 5)Armor Shop
    Armor Gacha (paid)Store purchase (check the price in the game)Armor Shop
    • The paid gachas have a ceiling: every 50 pulls for the Trap Gacha and every 50 pulls for the Armor Gacha (Glossary & FAQ).
    • Rates and lineups are on the Gacha page. The numbers are the real chances for your reached stage.
    • Armor comes in four parts (head, body, legs, accessory), and you wear one of each part. Each piece starts appearing at a set floor (see the armor detail pages).

    Gear & Upgrades (after clearing B4)

    • Traps and armor get stronger as their level goes up, and levels rise with EXP.
    • The EXP needed for the next level is 5 x the current level.
    • The level cap of a trap depends on its rarity. See the Upgrade Tables.
