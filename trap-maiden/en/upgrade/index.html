    Upgrade Tables

    Traps and armor get stronger as you raise their level with EXP. This page gathers the strength per level and the EXP needed. How each trap or armor changes is on its own page (traps, armor).

    How leveling works

    • The EXP needed for the next level is 5 x the current level.
    • The maximum level is 99.
    • A trap that reaches Lv50 turns gold and evolves.
    • EXP comes from clearing floors, missions (each pays 30% of the clear EXP of its floor), the Cave, Lily's House and more (see the Glossary & FAQ).
