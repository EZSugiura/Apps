    Chapter 2: B11-B20

    Map, enemy numbers, missions and rewards of each floor. Enemy numbers are the real values on that floor (level and stage scaling included).

    Mission checkmarks are saved only in this browser (they do not sync to other devices or to the game).

    Hard mode opens after clearing B10 and exists from B5. Enemies get +3 levels and hit harder.
