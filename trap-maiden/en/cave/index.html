    Training Cave

    A cave where you can replay the floors you have reached in the story. The map is generated randomly each time, and each floor is as strong as the same story floor.

    Rules

    UnlockThe cave opens when you clear story B8.
    Floors you can enterUp to the deepest floor you have cleared in the story (deepest: B120).
    MapGenerated randomly every time.
    Runs per day3 rewarded runs a day, +2 more by watching an ad (5 in all). From run 4 on it is practice: no rewards and the record does not change. The day changes with your device's date.
    Enemy levelThe enemy level of the same story floor +1.
    GemsA floor passed for the first time pays 5x gems. If you fall, you keep 50% of the gems.
    EXPA floor passed for the first time pays 1x the story clear EXP. Later clears pay 10% of that. Clearing at least one floor in a rewarded run also gives 2x the clear EXP of the deepest story floor you have reached.
    SkipAvailable after clearing story B30 and with a cave record of at least 1 floor. It uses one rewarded run and counts B1 up to your record as cleared without playing (gems and EXP at the later-clear rate; the record does not move).

    Enemies and rewards per floor

    Gems and EXP are per floor cleared. A floor you pass for the first time (deeper than your record) pays more gems. Enemies come from the pool of the area the floor belongs to.
