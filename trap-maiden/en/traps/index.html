    Trap list

    Basic Basic

    NameCategoryDescription
    Wooden Spikes
    		DamageSmall damage to the enemy that steps on it. Unlimited.

    N N

    NameCategoryDescription
    Bear Trap
    		BindSmall damage and holds the enemy for 3 turns. Heavy or slippery enemies break free.
    Sticky Sheet
    		BindHolds the enemy that steps on it for 5 turns. Useless on heavy or slippery enemies.
    Clapper
    		LureRattles when placed: every enemy on the floor heads here for 5 turns.
    Shallow Pit
    		DamageMedium damage and 1 turn of paralysis.
    Smoke Ball
    		StatusWhen an enemy steps on it, enemies in a 3x3 area lose sight of you for 3 turns and can't sense traps.
    Slippery Moss
    		MovementThe enemy slides 2 more cells the way it was going.
    Bait Bag
    		LureBeasts within 4 cells come to eat it for 2 turns. Even trap-sniffing beasts can't resist.
    Wooden Screen
    		DefenseBecomes a wall that also stops ranged attacks. Breaks after 3 hits.
    Oil Pot
    		StateCoats the enemy in oil for 5 turns. No damage, but fire traps then deal double damage and set it burning.
    Water Sack
    		StateSoaks a 3x3 area for 5 turns. Lightning traps deal 1.5x damage and paralyze for longer.
    Magnet Floor
    		BindHolds the metal bodies of machines and androids to the floor for 3 turns. Works on heavy ones; cleaner robots can't swallow it.
    Firecracker
    		StatusA bang and a flash: machines and androids in 3x3 lose their sensors for 3 turns, zombies are confused for 3 turns.
    Double-Bottom Trap
    		BindA small hit when stepped on. An enemy that tries to disarm or swallow it falls through: bound for 5 turns and a medium hit.
    Battery Detonator
    		DamageA small lightning hit and a turn of paralysis. Reaches holograms. x1.5 on a wet target.

    R R

    NameCategoryDescription
    Powder Keg
    		AreaExplodes when stepped on: medium damage to ground targets in 3x3, you included.
    Clockwork Bomb
    		DelayedExplodes 3 turns after placing: heavy damage in 3x3, hitting flyers too.
    Log Pendulum
    		DamageA log swings 3 cells the way it was placed: medium damage and a 1-cell push.
    Scarecrow
    		LureBecomes a scarecrow (15 HP). Enemies within 7 cells attack it before you.
    Sleep Herb Bag
    		StatusEveryone in 3x3 sleeps for 4 turns, you included. Damage wakes sleepers.
    Birdlime Net
    		Anti-airBrings a flying enemy down for 3 turns; ground traps work on it meanwhile.
    Oil Floor
    		MovementThe enemy slides until it hits a wall, triggering traps on the way.
    Rockfall
    		DamageMedium damage in a cross, 2 cells each way.
    Vibration Stake
    		Anti-burrowWhen a burrowing enemy comes within 2 cells, it's dragged up: heavy damage and stunned for 2 turns.
    Spin Floor
    		MovementTurns the enemy around and confuses it for 1 turn. Shields fail for 3 turns.
    Tripwire
    		ChainWhen an enemy touches the wire, every trap of yours within 3 cells goes off at once.
    Flint Trap
    		DamageSparks for small fire damage. Heavy damage and burning on oiled enemies; weak against soaked ones.
    Hanging Net
    		BindA net drops on the enemy that steps here and holds it. People-shaped enemies are held longest (zombies 6 turns, androids 6), others 3. Also holds heavy and slippery enemies.
    Iron Cage Trapdoor
    		BindA cage drops on the enemy that steps here and shuts it in for 8 turns. Works on heavy and shielded enemies; nothing gets out to attack. Bosses 3 turns.
    Neutralizing Mist
    		StatusA golden mist: clears the boosts of enemies in 3x3 and slows them for 3 turns (half speed, no healing).
    Jamming Box
    		StatusMachines and androids within 3 cells lose their trap sensors and ranged attacks for 5 turns.

    SR SR

    NameCategoryDescription
    Hydraulic Pitfall
    		AreaHeavy damage to ground targets in 2x2, you included. Instantly defeats heavyweight non-boss enemies.
    Spinning Saw
    		AreaMedium damage to ground targets in a 5-cell line the way it faces, you included. 3 uses.
    Discharge Plate
    		StatusMedium lightning damage and 2 turns of paralysis. 3 uses.
    EM Field
    		StatusSmall damage and 1 turn of paralysis in 3x3. Heavy damage to bodiless hologram enemies.
    Reflector Panel
    		DefenseA ranged attack that passes over this tile is bounced back.
    Air Shooter
    		MovementCarries the enemy through a tube and drops it on the last trap you placed.
    Camo Mine
    		StealthInvisible even to trap-spotting enemies. Medium damage to ground targets in 3x3, you included.
    Coolant Floor
    		BindFreezes ground targets in 3x3 for 4 turns, even heavy ones. You included.
    Sonic Music Box
    		LureEvery enemy in the room heads here for 5 turns.
    Shock Buoy
    		StatusPlace on water. When an enemy touches connected water, the whole area takes medium lightning damage and 2 turns of paralysis. You too, if you're in the water.
    Signal Scrambler
    		StatusZombies within 4 cells lose the key's orders: for 8 turns they stop working together and wander.

    SSR SSR

    NameCategoryDescription
    Hydraulic Press
    		AreaHeavy damage to everything in 5x5, flyers included, you included.
    Suction Cyclone
    		Movement2 turns after placing, pulls enemies within 4 cells to the center. You included.
    Restraint Field
    		BindBinds everything in 5x5 for 6 turns. Bosses for 3.
    Linked Switch
    		ChainWhen another trap goes off, it sets off every trap within 3 cells.
    Hologram Decoy
    		LureAlways active. Spawns 3 decoys that draw enemy aim (decoys have HP).
    EM Chain
    		BindBinds the enemy that steps on it for 10 turns, bosses too.
    Whirlpool Pump
    		AreaStepped on in water, it drags ground and water enemies in 3x3 under: instant defeat. Heavy damage to bosses. You included.

    UR UR

    NameCategoryDescription
    Silver Tail
    		SpecialA spring shoots a stake up to 6 cells: the first enemy takes a large hit and is bound for 4 turns, bosses too. Nothing sees it coming, disarms it or learns it.
    Gravity Collapser
    		AreaInstantly defeats everything in 3x3. Heavy damage to bosses.
    Area Stun Pulse
    		SpecialStops every enemy on the floor for 8 turns.
    Plasma Furnace
    		Delayed5 turns after placing, massive damage in 7x7. You are immune.
    Lure Shock Dome
    		LureAlways active. Draws in every enemy in the room and deals small damage each turn to those in range.
