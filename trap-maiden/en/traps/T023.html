    EM Field

    SR Status

    Small damage and 1 turn of paralysis in 3x3. Heavy damage to bodiless hologram enemies.

    In the margin of the blueprint: "We'll need this someday"

    Overview

    GachaNormal gacha (coins): from stage 101 (Banner 11, Banner 12)
    Paid gacha: from stage 1 (Banner 1, Banner 2, Banner 3, Banner 4, Banner 5, Banner 6)
    TriggerAn enemy steps on it
    Range3 x 3 square
    TargetsGround, Flying, Aquatic, Hologram
    Uses1
    PlacementLand
    OriginFather's design
    Max level99

    Effects

    • Damage: power 20
    • Paralyze: turns 1
    • Damage: power 100 (only vs Hologram)
