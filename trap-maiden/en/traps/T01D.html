    Iron Cage Trapdoor

    R Bind

    A cage drops on the enemy that steps here and shuts it in for 8 turns. Works on heavy and shielded enemies; nothing gets out to attack. Bosses 3 turns.

    A cage can protect, too

    Paid gacha: from stage 51 (Banner 3, Banner 4, Banner 5, Banner 6)

    Range1 cell
