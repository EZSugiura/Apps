    Oil Floor

    R Movement

    The enemy slides until it hits a wall, triggering traps on the way.

    A floor that delivers them to other traps

    Overview

    GachaNormal gacha (coins): from stage 21 (Banner 3, Banner 4, Banner 5, Banner 6, Banner 7, Banner 8, Banner 9, Banner 10, Banner 11, Banner 12)
    Paid gacha: from stage 21 (Banner 2, Banner 3, Banner 4, Banner 5, Banner 6)
    TriggerAn enemy steps on it
    Range1 cell
    TargetsGround
    Uses1
    PlacementLand
    OriginVillage
