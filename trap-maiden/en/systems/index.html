    Basic Systems

    Turns and enemy awareness

    Turns

    • Each time Lily acts once, the enemies act once.
    • Actions that take a turn: moving one tile, placing a trap, waiting in place, picking up a trap (one in front of you that has not triggered yet).
    • Actions that take no turn: turning, looking around the map.
    • Enemies differ in speed: some are slow, some normal, some fast (see "Speed" in the Enemies pages).
    • Every trap that triggers in the same turn counts as part of a "COMBO".

    The order of a turn:

    1. Lily acts
    2. Traps Lily stepped on are resolved
    3. Timed traps count down
    4. Enemies act (traps they step on are resolved too)
    5. The remaining turns of status effects go down
    6. If every enemy is defeated, the goal opens

    Enemy awareness

    • Until an enemy notices Lily, it wanders around.
    • Once it notices her, it chases her by the shortest path. If it loses her, it goes to where it last saw her and then goes back to wandering.
    • The usual sight range is 6 tiles. It differs by enemy; an enemy spots Lily when she is in the same room or on a straight line not blocked by walls.
    • On some floors the "chasing" enemies know where Lily is from the start and come at once. The "Noticing" column of each floor's enemy table tells you.
    • On some floors, fleeing enemies (rabbits and the like), stationary guards and bosses notice by sight.
    • An enemy blinded by a smoke bomb or similar sees nothing.
    • When there is a lure trap (clapper, feast bag and so on), enemies go for the trap first. The priority is: status effect > lure or feast > decoy > Lily.
    • Enemies normally do not avoid traps. Enemies that can spot traps do avoid the ones nearby.
    • Status effects such as sleep and bind last only 50% as long on bosses.

    Enemy level

    • Each enemy level adds 18% to its HP.
    • How hard an enemy hits does not change with level. It is set per floor.

    Lily's HP

    • Her max HP is the starting 120 plus whatever the armor you wear adds.
    • HP does not recover over time. Heal with ads or armor skills.

    Terrain

    Maps have rock walls, low walls and puddles. Ordinary traps cannot be placed on any of them.

    TerrainLilyWalking enemiesFlying enemiesLine of sight
    Rock wallCannot passCannot passCannot passBlocks it. Stops straight-line traps and sweeping attacks too
    Low wallCannot passCannot pass (jumping enemies cross it)Cross itDoes not block
    PuddleCannot passCannot enter (enemies that move through water, and jumpers, can)Can enterDoes not block
    • Rock wall: nobody can pass. You can also hide behind it.
    • Low wall: it stops only walking enemies. Enemies that cross it: Timid Rabbit, Ratling, Hopping Frog.
    • Puddle: enemies that can move through water: Glow Slime, Hopping Frog.
    • Some armor skills let Lily wade through water or climb over low walls.
    • Some traps can only be placed on water.

    COMBO and CHAIN

    Two mechanics produce big damage: COMBO and CHAIN. They can trigger at the same time.

    COMBO (traps in the same turn)

    • It is the number of traps that triggered within one turn: the first, the second, the third and so on.
    • An element reaction (fire on oil, lightning on wet, physical on frozen) adds to the COMBO.
    • The second trap and later deal more damage. See the table below.
    • Every trap that triggers that turn counts, whether Lily placed it or an enemy stepped on it.

    CHAIN (consecutive turns)

    • A single enemy caught by traps again on the next turn, without a break, builds a CHAIN.
    • The first turn is 1; if it is caught again next turn it is 2, then 3 and so on.
    • Turns when the enemy is held by a trap and cannot act (bind, frozen, paralysis, sleep, stun and so on) do not break the CHAIN, but the number does not rise.
    • A single turn without a trap hit resets it to 1.
    • It is counted per enemy.

    From 5 COMBO on, the multiplier stays at x3 (the cap).

    CHAIN multiplier (how many turns in a row):

    CHAINMultiplier
    1x1
    2x1.2
    3x1.4
    4x1.6
    5x2
    6x2

    From 5 CHAIN on, the multiplier stays at x2 (the cap).

    • Some armor skills add to the COMBO multiplier (up to +0.2).
    • COMBO and CHAIN apply only to trap damage, not to burning and similar damage.

    Damage formula

    Damage of one trap = trap power x element multiplier x COMBO multiplier x CHAIN multiplier (rounded)
    • Trap power comes in four basic tiers: small 20, medium 48, large 100, huge 200.
    • The rarity of the trap multiplies it: N x1, R x1.1, SR x1.3, SSR x1.5, UR x1.8.
    • Each level adds about 5% more (see the Upgrade Tables).
    • Element multipliers: fire on an oiled enemy x2 (and burning for 3 turns), lightning on a wet enemy x1.5 (1 turn of paralysis), physical on a frozen enemy x1.5. Burning does 3 damage per turn.

    Worked example

    Say 3 traps of rarity N, medium power, Lv1 hit one enemy in the same turn. That enemy was also caught by a trap the turn before (CHAIN 2). Element reactions and armor skills are ignored.

    • Trap power: 48 (medium) x 1 (N) = 48
    • CHAIN is 2, so x1.2
    OrderCOMBOCalculationDamage
    Trap 1148 × 1 × 1.2 = 57.658
    Trap 2248 × 1.5 × 1.2 = 86.486
    Trap 3348 × 2 × 1.2 = 115.2115

    The total is 259. With no CHAIN or COMBO, the same trap would deal 48 once. Chained together, the same traps do 5 times as much or more.

    On screen

    • Two lines appear in the middle of the screen: "n COMBO" (gold) on top and "n CHAIN" (light blue) below. If only one applies, only that line shows.
    • On a turn when several traps triggered, "TOTAL" and the trap damage of that turn appear under the enemy.
    • The mission "deal N damage in one combo" counts the total damage dealt to the same enemy in that combo (a turn with only a CHAIN counts too).
