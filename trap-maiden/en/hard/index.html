    Hard Mode

    Hard Mode lets you replay story floors with the same terrain but stronger enemies, and it pays more.

    Unlock

    • After clearing B10, a "Normal / Hard" switch appears on the stage select screen.
    • Hard floors start from B5. You can challenge only floors you have cleared on Normal.

    Differences from Normal

    ItemIn Hard Mode
    Terrain and enemy kindsSame as Normal
    Enemy level+3
    Enemy hitsx1.5
    Clear gemsx3 (the extra first-clear bonus is 50; on Normal it is 15)
    Clear EXP (first time)x2 of Normal
    Mission gemsx3
    Target of the mission "deal N damage in one combo"x1.5 (rounded up)
    Set rewardsClear a set of floors together for traps or loadout slots (tables below)

    The mission kinds and their order are the same as on Normal. Counts such as "no damage" or "defeat N" do not change. The Hard missions of each floor are on the Story pages.

    Examples of gems and EXP for a clear

    The Normal clear reward is 10 + 1 x the floor number, plus 15 the first time. On Hard it is the Normal clear reward x3, plus 50 the first time.
