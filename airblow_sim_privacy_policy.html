Air Blow Simulator

Privacy Policy / プライバシーポリシー

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Summary: Air Blow Simulator does not collect, store, or share the piping designs and calculations you enter — those are processed and saved locally on your device. We display ads through Google AdMob, which may collect certain advertising-related data as described below. If you choose to purchase the paid Pro upgrade (subscription, one-time purchase, or 3-day pass) it is processed entirely by Google Play. If you additionally sign in with Google — to use the ad-free, Pro-only Web version at the same time as the Android app — we receive your Google account's email address, display name, and account identifier, and store a record of your Pro entitlement linked to that identifier so the same purchase unlocks both.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how EZ Company ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile application Air Blow Simulator (the "App"). The App is an engineering tool for designing compressed-air blow-nozzle piping systems and simulating pressure loss, flow rate, and impact-pressure distribution. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

All piping designs, pressures, dimensions, and other values you enter into the App are processed and stored entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of this data on external servers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information (via Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may automatically collect the following information to serve relevant ads:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device type, operating system version, and language settings
  • App interaction data (e.g., ad impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad views)
  • Diagnostic data

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC in accordance with their own privacy policy. We do not have direct access to or control over this data.

2.3 Purchases (Pro subscription, one-time purchase, or 3-day pass)

The App offers an optional paid "Pro" upgrade (a monthly/yearly auto-renewing subscription, a one-time lifetime purchase, or a non-renewing 3-day pass) that removes ads and unlocks the Web version described below. All purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not operate our own payment servers and never see your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.). The App only reads your entitlement status (whether a purchase is currently active) from Google Play, or — for the 3-day pass — from an expiry timestamp stored locally on your device, to decide whether to hide ads and enable the Web version.

2.4 Google Sign-In (optional, only if you use the Web version)

The App itself does not require an account. If you choose to sign in with Google — solely to use the same Pro purchase from the companion Web version in your browser — we receive your Google account's email address, display name, and account identifier via Firebase Authentication. This is used only to identify which account you are using; we do not use it for advertising or any other purpose.

2.5 Pro entitlement sync (Android ⇄ Web, via Firebase Cloud Firestore)

If you purchase Pro on Android and sign in with Google, we store a record — linked to your Google account identifier — in Firebase Cloud Firestore indicating that your account has an active Pro entitlement (including the product/purchase type, the 3-day pass's expiry time if applicable, and an update timestamp). This record does not contain your piping designs, calculation data, or payment details. It exists solely so the same Pro purchase can unlock the ad-free Web version when you sign in there with the same Google account.

2.6 Web version

The App has a companion browser-based Web version for Pro users. The Web version shows no ads and stores your piping designs only in your browser's local storage — it is never transmitted to us. Visiting the Web version without an active Pro entitlement shows only a sign-in/upgrade screen.

3. How We Use Information

We do not use or process any personal information directly. The advertising data collected by Google AdMob is used by Google for:

  • Delivering and personalizing advertisements
  • Measuring ad performance
  • Fraud prevention

4. Data Storage

The App stores your saved piping designs and preferences locally on your device using the operating system's standard local storage (SharedPreferences). You may save a limited number of designs, and watching a rewarded ad may be required to save additional designs. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — advertising platform operated by Google LLC.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    Opt-out: You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking).
  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication (Google Sign-In) and Cloud Firestore (Pro entitlement sync), used only if you sign in to use the Web version. See Firebase and privacy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes subscription, one-time, and 3-day pass Pro purchases.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from Google Play > Subscriptions. You can sign out of your Google account at any time from the App's settings, which stops the Pro entitlement sync described above without affecting your Android purchase itself.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • The right to access information about data collected about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of personalized advertising

Since we do not collect personal data directly, most requests should be directed to Google LLC for AdMob-related data.

8. Data Security

All design and calculation data remains on your device and is never transmitted. We take reasonable measures to protect the App from security vulnerabilities, though no software is completely immune to risk.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
