Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-30

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app Psychrometric Chart (Android package psychrometricchart.EZCompany) and its companion Web version (a browser-based version of the app for Pro users, available at psychrometric-chart-bdf76.web.app) collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you. The app and Web version are developed and published by an individual developer ("we", "us").

Summary

  • If you tap "Use current location" in the paid HVAC weather-simulation feature, the app requests your device's approximate (coarse) location once, to look up your time zone and pre-fill coordinates for the weather lookup. Outside of that button, the app never accesses device location.
  • Your saved chart points and settings are stored only on your device (or, for the Web version, only in your browser).
  • Advertising (Google AdMob) and basic usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) are used to support the free Android version. The Web version shows no ads.
  • If you use the optional "Contact Us" form, the message and the information you choose to provide are sent to us via Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • If you purchase Pro (subscription or one-time purchase), the purchase itself is processed entirely by Google Play; we only read your entitlement status from Google Play.
  • If you sign in with Google (from Settings, or to use the Web version), we receive your Google account's email address, display name, and account identifier, and store a record of your Pro entitlement status linked to that identifier so the same purchase can unlock both the Android app and the Web version.

Information We Collect

CategoryDetails
Device/location data Requested only when you tap "Use current location" in the paid HVAC weather-simulation feature (approximate/coarse location permission). The position obtained is used on-device to find the nearest reference city for your time zone, and — like any coordinates you enter or select manually — is sent to NASA POWER (see Third-Party Services below) to fetch weather data. This is stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore and used only to respond to you and improve the app.
Purchases / subscriptions Subscription and one-time (lifetime) Pro purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not operate our own payment servers and do not see your payment details. The app reads your entitlement status from Google Play to unlock the ad-free experience and paid features.
Google Sign-In If you sign in with Google — from the Android app's Settings screen, or when using the Web version — we receive your Google account's email address, display name, and account identifier via Firebase Authentication. This is used only to identify which account you are using; we do not use it for advertising or any other purpose.
Pro entitlement sync (Android ⇄ Web) If you purchase Pro on Android and sign in with Google, we store a record — linked to your Google account identifier — in Firebase Cloud Firestore indicating that your account has an active Pro entitlement, along with the product/purchase type and update timestamp. This record does not contain your chart data, saved points, or payment details. It exists solely so the same Pro purchase can unlock the Web version when you sign in there with the same Google account.
Locally saved data Chart points, unit system, language, and display preferences are stored only on your device (or, for the Web version, only in your browser's local storage) and are not transmitted to us.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising. See how Google uses information from sites/apps that use its services.
  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication (Google Sign-In), Analytics, and Cloud Firestore (contact form storage and Pro entitlement sync). See Firebase and privacy.
  • Google Play Billing — subscription and one-time Pro purchases.

The app's optional paid feature fetches historical weather data for a location you choose — by searching a city, entering coordinates manually, or tapping "Use current location" — from NASA POWER. In all three cases, the latitude/longitude (your device's actual GPS position only in the "Use current location" case) is sent to NASA's public API to retrieve weather data.

Your Choices

  • You can manage ad personalization consent from the app's Settings screen ("Privacy Options"), where available for your region.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from Google Play > Subscriptions.
  • You can sign out of your Google account at any time from the app's Settings screen ("Account" section). This stops the Pro entitlement sync described above; it does not affect your Android purchase itself.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device. To request deletion of the Pro entitlement record linked to your Google account, or of a contact-form submission you previously sent us, please contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app's features change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, please use the in-app Menu > Settings > Contact Us form.

概要

  • 有償の空調シミュレーション機能で「現在地から取得」ボタンを押した時のみ、端末のおおよその位置情報を一度取得し、タイムゾーンの特定と気象データ取得用の座標入力に使います。このボタンを使わない限り、端末の位置情報にはアクセスしません。
  • 保存したポイントや各種設定は端末内(Web版の場合はブラウザ内)にのみ保存されます。
  • 無料のAndroid版の運営のため、広告(Google AdMob)と簡易な利用状況分析(Firebase Analytics)を利用しています。Web版に広告は表示されません。
  • アプリ内の「お問い合わせ」フォームを利用した場合、入力内容はFirebase Cloud Firestoreを通じて当方へ送信されます。
  • Pro版(サブスクリプションまたは買い切り)を購入した場合、決済処理自体はGoogle Playが行い、当方はGoogle Playから権利状態のみを取得します。
  • Googleでサインインした場合(設定画面から、またはWeb版利用時)、Googleアカウントのメールアドレス・表示名・アカウント識別子を取得し、Android版とWeb版の両方で同じPro購入を有効にできるよう、その識別子に紐づけてPro権利の状態を保存します。
