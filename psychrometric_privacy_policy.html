Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-19

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app Psychrometric Chart (Android package psychrometricchart.EZCompany) collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. The app is developed and published by an individual developer ("we", "us").

Summary

  • If you tap "Use current location" in the paid HVAC weather-simulation feature, the app requests your device's approximate (coarse) location once, to look up your time zone and pre-fill coordinates for the weather lookup. Outside of that button, the app never accesses device location.
  • Your saved chart points and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob) and basic usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) are used to support the free version.
  • If you use the optional "Contact Us" form, the message and the information you choose to provide are sent to us via Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • If you purchase a subscription, the purchase itself is processed entirely by Google Play; we only read your subscription status from Google Play.

Information We Collect

CategoryDetails
Device/location data Requested only when you tap "Use current location" in the paid HVAC weather-simulation feature (approximate/coarse location permission). The position obtained is used on-device to find the nearest reference city for your time zone, and — like any coordinates you enter or select manually — is sent to NASA POWER (see Third-Party Services below) to fetch weather data. It is not stored or otherwise collected by us.
Advertising data Banner, app-open, and rewarded ads are served through Google AdMob (google_mobile_ads). AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and interaction data to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and applicable US states, we show Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before requesting ads.
Usage analytics We use Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage in aggregate. This does not include the contents of your saved data or chart points.
Contact form submissions If you use the in-app "Contact Us" form, we receive the category, your message, the app version/platform/language, and any name or reply email address you choose to enter (both optional). This is stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore and used only to respond to you and improve the app.
Purchases / subscriptions Subscription purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not operate our own payment servers and do not see your payment details. The app reads your subscription status from Google Play to unlock the ad-free experience and, in the future, paid features.
Locally saved data Chart points, unit system, language, and display preferences are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising. See how Google uses information from sites/apps that use its services.
  • Firebase (Google) — Analytics and Cloud Firestore (contact form storage). See Firebase and privacy.
  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchases.

The app's optional paid feature fetches historical weather data for a location you choose — by searching a city, entering coordinates manually, or tapping "Use current location" — from NASA POWER. In all three cases, the latitude/longitude (your device's actual GPS position only in the "Use current location" case) is sent to NASA's public API to retrieve weather data.

Your Choices

  • You can manage ad personalization consent from the app's Settings screen ("Privacy Options"), where available for your region.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from Google Play > Subscriptions.
  • Since we do not maintain user accounts, there is no separate account-deletion process. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device. To request deletion of a contact-form submission you previously sent us, please contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app's features change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, please use the in-app Menu > Settings > Contact Us form.

概要

  • 有償の空調シミュレーション機能で「現在地から取得」ボタンを押した時のみ、端末のおおよその位置情報を一度取得し、タイムゾーンの特定と気象データ取得用の座標入力に使います。このボタンを使わない限り、端末の位置情報にはアクセスしません。
  • 保存したポイントや各種設定は端末内にのみ保存されます。
  • 無料版の運営のため、広告(Google AdMob)と簡易な利用状況分析(Firebase Analytics)を利用しています。
  • アプリ内の「お問い合わせ」フォームを利用した場合、入力内容はFirebase Cloud Firestoreを通じて当方へ送信されます。
  • サブスクリプションを購入した場合、決済処理自体はGoogle Playが行い、当方はGoogle Playから購読状態のみを取得します。

取得する情報

区分詳細
端末の位置情報 有償の空調シミュレーション機能で「現在地から取得」を押した時のみ取得します (おおよその位置情報の権限)。取得した位置は、端末内でタイムゾーン特定用の最寄り 都市を探すために使われ、手入力・都市選択した座標と同様にNASA POWER(下記 「第三者サービス」参照)へ気象データ取得のため送信されます。当方が保存・収集する ことはありません。
広告関連データ バナー・アプリ起動時・リワード広告はGoogle AdMob(google_mobile_ads)経由で 配信されます。AdMobは広告の配信・効果測定のため広告IDや操作データを取得する場合が あります。EEA・英国・スイス、および対象となる米国州では、広告リクエスト前にGoogleの User Messaging Platform(UMP)による同意フォームを表示します。
利用状況の分析 Firebase Analyticsにより、機能の利用状況を集計的に把握しています。保存データや ポイントの内容自体は含まれません。
お問い合わせフォームの内容 アプリ内の「お問い合わせ」フォームを利用した場合、種別・本文・アプリバージョン/ プラットフォーム/言語、および任意入力のお名前・返信用メールアドレスを受け取ります。 Firebase Cloud Firestoreに保存され、返信とアプリ改善のみに利用します。
購入・サブスクリプション サブスクリプションの購入処理はGoogle Play Billingが全て行います。当方は独自の決済 サーバーを持たず、決済情報自体を確認することはできません。アプリは広告非表示および 将来の有償機能を有効化するため、Google Playから購読状態のみを読み取ります。
端末内保存データ ポイント・単位系・表示言語・表示設定は端末内にのみ保存され、当方へ送信されません。
